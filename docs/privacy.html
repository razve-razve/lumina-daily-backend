Privacy Policy

Lumina Daily  ·  Last updated: April 5, 2026

Overview

Lumina Daily ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal astrology app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Data We Collect

To provide personalised daily readings, we collect the following information you provide during onboarding:

  • Email address (used only for account authentication)
  • First name
  • Gender (optional)
  • Date and time of birth
  • City of birth (latitude, longitude, timezone)

We also collect:

  • Device push notification token (only if you enable notifications)
  • App language preference
  • Subscription status (managed by Apple)

How We Use Your Data

  • Birth data is used solely to calculate your natal chart and generate personalised daily astrological readings. It is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Email is used only for account login via Supabase. We do not send marketing emails.
  • Push token is used only to deliver your daily reading notification at the time you choose.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on servers provided by Supabase, hosted in the EU. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. We retain your data for as long as your account is active.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
  • OpenAI — AI text generation for daily readings (birth data is not sent to OpenAI; only astrological chart data such as sign positions and transit aspects is used)
  • Apple — in-app purchases (subscriptions) and push notifications
  • Railway — backend server hosting

Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app: Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Children's Privacy

Lumina Daily is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Significant changes will be communicated via the app. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any revisions.

Contact

Questions or requests regarding your data? Email us at luminadaily@yahoo.com.
